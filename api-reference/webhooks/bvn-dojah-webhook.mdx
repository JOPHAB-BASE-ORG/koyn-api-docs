## How it works

Dojah calls this route when an identity verification completes. The route is exempt from the client-version gate. For a BVN or NIN verification, Koyn acknowledges receipt and dispatches a queued face-match job that compares the submitted selfie against the government record and updates the user's KYC status and tier. Other verification types are acknowledged without further processing.
