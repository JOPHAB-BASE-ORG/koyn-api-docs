title: "Purchase Airtime"
api: "POST /phonebill/purchase-airtime"
---

## How it works

This debits your wallet (naira balance) and queues an airtime top-up with the provider. Your account must be active and unflagged, the request must come from a verified device, and the `bills` service switch must be on, or the request is rejected with `409`. It also fails when your balance is below the amount or a matching top-up is already in flight.

## Body

<ParamField body="network" type="string" required>
  ISP product code, for example `MTN`. Must match an active provider.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="amount" type="number" required>
  Amount in naira. Minimum `100`. Example: `100`
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="phone_no" type="string" required>
  Recipient phone number, digits only. Example: `08012345678`
</ParamField>
