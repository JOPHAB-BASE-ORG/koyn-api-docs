title: "Meter Purchase"
api: "POST /meter/purchase"
---

## How it works

This debits your wallet (naira balance) and queues an electricity token purchase with the provider; a prepaid token is returned on the top-up record once the provider approves. Verify the meter first to obtain the `customer_name`. Your account must be active and unflagged, the request must come from a verified device, and the `bills` service switch must be on, or the request is rejected with `409`; it also fails when your balance is below the amount or a matching purchase is already in flight.

## Body

<ParamField body="product" type="string" required>
  Meter product name, for example `Eko`. Must match an active provider.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="meter_no" type="string" required>
  Meter number, at least 10 digits. Example: `0000000000`
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="meter_type" type="string" required>
  One of `PREPAID` or `POSTPAID`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="customer_name" type="string" required>
  Account holder name returned by meter verification. Example: `JOHN DOE`
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="amount" type="number" required>
  Amount in naira. Minimum `500`. Example: `1000`
</ParamField>
