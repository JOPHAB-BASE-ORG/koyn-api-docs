---
title: "Fund Bet Account"
api: "POST /betting/fund"
---

## How it works

This funds a betting account and debits your wallet (naira balance) for `amount` plus the betting service charge. The response records the `charge` and `total_amount` (`amount + charge`). Fetch the current fee from `GET /bills/service-charges` before you show the total. Your account must be active and unflagged, the request must come from a verified device, and the `bills` service switch must be on, or the request is rejected with `409`; it also fails when your balance cannot cover `amount + charge` or a matching top-up is already in flight.

## Body

<ParamField body="product" type="string" required>
  Betting product code, for example `BET9JA`. Must match an active provider.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="customer_id" type="string" required>
  Betting account ID, digits only. Example: `3308882`
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="phone_number" type="string" required>
  Account phone number. Example: `08012345678`
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="amount" type="number" required>
  Amount in naira to fund. Your balance must cover this plus the service charge.
</ParamField>
