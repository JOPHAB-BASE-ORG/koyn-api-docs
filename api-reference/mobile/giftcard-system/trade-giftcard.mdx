---
title: "Trade GiftCard"
api: "POST /giftcards/trades"
---

## How it works

You submit a gift card for sale against a chosen brand category. You must provide a `code`, one or more `images`, or both; if the category requires a receipt, images are mandatory. The `amount` must fall within the category's `min` and `max` range, and the category and its brand must be active. On success the trade is created with status `pending` and a matching pending wallet credit is recorded for the calculated payout (`amount` x `rate`); the balance is not released until an admin approves the trade. To preview the payout before submitting, call `POST /giftcards/trades/calculate` with `category_id` and `amount`.

## Body

<Note>Send as `multipart/form-data`.</Note>

<ParamField body="category_id" type="integer" required>
  Brand category `id` from `GET /giftcards/brands/{giftcardBrand}/categories`. Example: `1`
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="amount" type="number" required>
  Card value to sell. Must be at least `0.01` and within the category's `min`–`max` range. Example: `50`
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="code" type="string">
  Gift card code. Up to 255 characters and unique per user. Required if no images are sent. Example: `HD3DSDHUY46`
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="images[]" type="file">
  Card or receipt image(s). 1 to 5 files, each `jpeg`, `png`, `jpg`, or `webp`, up to 20 MB. Required if no code is sent, and required when the category has `requires_receipt`.
</ParamField>
