## How it works

This debits your naira wallet and creates a withdrawal to one of your saved bank accounts. Your account must be active and the request must come from a safe device, or it is rejected with `403`; you must also have a verified BVN and pass your 4-digit transaction PIN. The amount must be at least ₦5,000, no more than your balance, and within your tier's daily withdrawal limit, and you cannot start another withdrawal within 5 minutes of your last one. Depending on system settings and whether your account is flagged for review, the payout is either sent automatically or held for manual approval.

The bank account must belong to you: its name is resolved with the bank provider and matched against your verified BVN name. A third-party account, an unresolvable account, or a missing verified identity returns `422` and no withdrawal is created, no wallet is debited, and no payout is queued. The saved account name on file cannot authorize a payout on its own.

## Body

<ParamField body="amount" type="number" required>
  Amount in naira. Minimum `5000`, capped by your balance and daily tier limit. Example: `2500`
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="pin" type="string" required>
  Your 4-digit transaction PIN. Example: `0000`
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="bank_id" type="number" required>
  Id of one of your saved bank accounts to pay out to. Example: `4`
</ParamField>
